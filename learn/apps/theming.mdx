Create custom themes to control the visual appearance of your apps. Themes are defined at the organization level and can be applied to individual apps.

## Themes

Go to **Organization Settings > Branding** to manage themes.

- **Create themes** — define color schemes for your apps
- **Set a default** — new apps automatically use the default theme
- **Duplicate** — copy an existing theme as a starting point
- **Preview** — see a live preview of how the theme looks

Each app can use a different theme, or you can set a default that applies to all new apps.

### Color customization

Customize your theme colors using color pickers:

- **Base color** — the primary color used throughout your app (adjustable via Hue + Saturation)
- **Theme color** — an accent or secondary color (adjustable via Hue + Saturation)

This gives you precise control over your app's visual appearance without being limited to predefined colors.

### Elevation & shadows

Configure how shadows and depth appear on cards and UI elements. These settings control the visual hierarchy and polish of your interface.

### Upgrading themes

When Major releases new theme features or improvements, you can upgrade your existing themes to the latest version. Upgrades preserve your custom colors and settings while applying new capabilities.

## Logos

Upload your organization's branding:

- **Full logo** — displayed in the app header and navigation
- **Icon logo** — used as a favicon and in compact views
