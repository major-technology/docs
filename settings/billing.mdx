Major uses a credit-based billing system. Credits (denominated in USD) are consumed by AI coding sessions, hosted browser time, AI proxy usage, and app deployments.

## Free Tier

Every organization starts on the free tier with:

- **$2.00 in daily credits** - reset at midnight UTC
- **2 apps** maximum
- **2 deployed apps** maximum
- **1 builder** (users who can create apps)
- **3 viewers** (users with member access)

## Team Plans

Team plans increase limits to **15 builders**, **50 viewers**, and unlimited apps. Plans are available in monthly and annual billing intervals.

| Plan | Credits / month |
| --- | --- |
| $100 | $100 |
| $200 | $200 |
| $400 | $408 |
| $800 | $824 |
| $1,200 | $1,248 |
| $1,600 | $1,680 |
| $2,000 | $2,120 |
| $2,400 | $2,568 |

Plans are billed monthly or annually.

Annual plans include a **20% credit bonus** on top of the monthly balance. When you select an annual plan, the pricing display breaks down the cost as the base monthly price plus the bonus credit value, making the total benefit more transparent. Unused credits roll over for 30 days on monthly plans and 365 days on annual plans.

Team plans also enable **domain auto-enrollment** - new users who sign up with your organization's email domain are automatically added. Users who sign up with an email and password are added once they verify their email address.

## Trials

If your organization is on a trial, every member sees a reminder in the web app during the trial's last 7 days, saying when it ends. Click **Got it** to dismiss it; it comes back every 4 hours until the trial ends. After the trial ends, access may be revoked for all members of the organization. To extend a trial, contact your account executive.

## Wallet & Credits

Your organization has a wallet that holds available credits. Credits are spent during:
- **Coding Sessions** - AI-powered app building and modifications
- **Browser** - hosted [browser](/learn/agents/browser) time used by the Platform Agent and agents
- **AI Proxy** - API requests through Major's managed LLM gateway
- **App Compute** - running deployed applications

### App Compute

Each deployed app is metered hourly, priced from its CPU and memory limits, and charged only for the minutes its server was running in that hour. An always-on app pays for the full hour. An app that [stops when idle](/learn/apps/deploying#stop-when-idle) pays nothing for the time it's stopped, and a frontend-only app with no server isn't charged for app compute.

### When your balance runs out

Starting a coding session, or sending a new message in one, requires a positive balance.

On a paid plan (Team or Enterprise), a coding session that is already running can take the wallet down to **-$5.00**, so an in-progress turn isn't cut off at exactly $0. Free-tier organizations have no overdraft: coding sessions stop at $0.

AI proxy calls from deployed apps stop at $0 on every plan.

### Overage Credits

When your balance reaches zero, you can purchase additional credits (overage). Overage credits are applied to your wallet immediately.

If your account has accrued debt from overage usage, purchasing new credits automatically forgives the negative balance and credits your wallet with the full purchase amount.

### Auto-Refill

Enable auto-refill in settings to automatically purchase credits when your balance drops below a threshold. Set your target balance and maximum monthly spending to stay within budget.

### Credit Precision

Credit amounts support up to 6 decimal places to accommodate sub-cent AI inference costs. Your wallet balance and transaction history reflect this precision.

## Referrals

Refer another company to Major and both organizations get **$100 in credit**. Open **Settings > Referrals**, or click **Refer a team, get $100** in the account menu or at the bottom of the sidebar. Every member can open it, whatever their role.

Each member has one referral link per organization. From the Referrals page you can copy or edit your link, invite people by email, and share it to LinkedIn, X, Slack, or email. A table lists everyone you've referred and where each referral stands, and lets you resend or cancel an email invite.

How a referral earns the reward:

1. Someone opens your link or your email invite and signs up for Major. If they open several people's links, the last one they opened within 60 days counts.
2. They create a **new organization**. Joining an organization that already exists, including yours, isn't a referral, so referrals only count for companies new to Major.
3. Their organization pays its first Team plan invoice.
4. 14 days after that payment, if it wasn't refunded or disputed, $100 is added to both organizations' wallets. A payment that is refunded or disputed in those 14 days reverses the reward, and the Referrals page tells you so.

Referral credit never expires. Later invoices don't add more, and a refund after the 14 days doesn't take the credit back.

The Referrals page and the reward emails also offer a **$100 Amazon gift card** on top of the credit.

## Usage Dashboard

View your monthly spending broken down by category:

- **Coding Sessions** - AI coder agent usage
- **AI Proxy** - LLM API calls through the managed proxy
- **App Compute** - deployed application infrastructure

Go to **Organization Settings > Usage** to see a daily breakdown with charts and summary cards. You can navigate between months to track spending trends.

On the agents dashboard under **Analytics**, agent spend is split into **Model**, **Compute**, and **Browser**.

### Frontend App Usage Tracking

Major tracks both anonymous and authenticated user access to your deployed frontend applications. When users authenticate and access your apps, Major records usage metrics and last-seen timestamps. This data powers your analytics and helps track active users for billing purposes.

## Managing Your Plan

1. Go to **Organization Settings > Billing**
2. View your current plan, credit balance, and usage
3. Click **Upgrade** to switch to a team plan or adjust your subscription

Billing is managed through Stripe Checkout. You can update payment methods, view invoices, and manage your subscription from the billing settings.
